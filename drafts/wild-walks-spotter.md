A small web app that creates printable spotter sheets for nature walks. Pick a location, time of year, and region, and it generates a sheet of things to look out for on your walk.

## What It Does

You choose where you're walking and the time of year, and it builds a custom spotter list — things like birds, plants, tracks, and other wildlife you might spot. The sheet is designed to be printed and taken along on the walk.

## Features

- Choose your walk location and time of year
- Select a UK region for locally relevant wildlife
- Optional explorer name for kids
- Grayscale-friendly output for black and white printing
- Generates a clean, printable sheet ready to take outdoors
