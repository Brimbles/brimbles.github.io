Three-String Songbook

2026-11-22

A web app for learning very easy songs on a 3-string guitar like the Loog. It starts with songs on a single string and works up to songs that use all three.

What It Does

Pick a song and the notes slide along a horizontal fretboard towards a play line, each one as long as the note it plays. The app plays each note as it reaches the line, and an animal band (borrowed from my Brush Buddies app) plays along.

Turn on listening and it hears the guitar through the microphone, so only the notes actually played score points.

Features

  • Play along: the notes scroll in time with the band
  • Practice: the fretboard waits until it hears the right note
  • Listen first: hear how the song goes before playing it
  • Any tuning: the songs are re-fingered to fit, with a built-in tuner
  • Stars, points and ranks, saved on the device
  • 13 songs, from Hot Cross Buns to In the Hall of the Mountain King
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers
