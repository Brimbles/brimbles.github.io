Gwdihŵ, the tawny owl

A cuddly talking owl for a three-year-old. The outside is soft fabric with flapping little wings, a printed beak, LCD eyes under polycarbonate domes, and printed eyelids that blink. Inside is a tough PETG skeleton powered by 8 AA batteries.

Soft outside

Removable minky skin with felt feather appliqué. Head and body skins are separate; the neck ruff hides the gap when the head turns.

Hard inside

PETG skeleton carries all loads. No magnets, no small parts that can come loose, and the battery lid needs a screwdriver.

Bottom-heavy

About 220 g of AA cells sit in the base (room for a second pack), so the owl rights itself like a weighted toy and lands base-first.

Concept illustration only, not to scale. Proportions will change once the CAD skeleton exists.
